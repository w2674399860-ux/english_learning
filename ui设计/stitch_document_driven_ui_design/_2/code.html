英文短文

Every autumn, Mia walked through the forest to help her grandmother with the apple harvest. This year, the journey felt different. A silver lantern hung by the old bridge, and someone had painted the window of the ancient mill bright yellow. Mia was not very brave, but she made a promise to find out who did it. She followed the river into the quiet valley. Near the mountain path, she heard a gentle whisper. It was her grandmother, smiling. “You found my surprise,” she said. “I wanted the village to feel warm again.” Mia laughed. She realized that courage is not about being fearless. It is about taking the next step, even when your hands are shaking.

中文地道翻译

双语对照

每年秋天，米娅都会穿过森林 (forest)，去帮奶奶收获 (harvest) 苹果 (apple)。今年的旅程 (journey) 却有些不同。一盏银色的 (silver) 灯笼 (lantern) 挂在旧桥 (bridge) 边，有人把古老的 (ancient) 磨坊的窗户 (window) 涂成了明亮的黄色 (yellow)。米娅并不怎么勇敢 (brave)，但她许下承诺 (promise)，一定要找出是谁做的。她顺着河 (river) 走进安静的山谷 (valley)。在山 (mountain) 路旁，她听到一声温柔的 (gentle) 低语 (whisper)。原来是奶奶，正笑着看她。“你发现了我的惊喜，”奶奶说，“我想让村子重新变得温暖。”米娅笑了。她明白了，勇气 (courage) 并不是毫无畏惧，而是即使双手发抖，也愿意迈出下一步。
